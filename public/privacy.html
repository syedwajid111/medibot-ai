Privacy policy

پرائیویسی پالیسی

Last updated: October 2026

MediBot is a student project that gives general health information. We collect as little as possible and only to make the service work for you.

What we store

  • Chats: your questions and MediBot's answers, so you can see your history. Turn on Private mode in the chat to not save a conversation.
  • Symptom checks, reminders and lab-tool usage you choose to save.
  • Account details (name, email, a securely hashed password, and optional profile such as age group) if you create an account.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (for example, which topics are asked most) to improve the knowledge base.
  • Contact form messages you send to the team.

Who can see it

Authorised project staff can review conversations in the staff panel to check answer quality and improve the knowledge base. Guests appear only as a random ID; registered users by email.

What we don't do

  • We don't store your IP address with your chats, sell data, or show ads.
  • Your location for "Nearby care" stays in your browser; it is sent only to OpenStreetMap to find places.
  • Don't enter your CNIC, address, phone number or other identifying details in chats.
  • Lab report photos and PDFs you upload are processed in memory and not stored; they are sent to the AI provider only to read the values.

Third-party services

  • The AI provider (Groq, or Google Gemini as a backup) processes the text of your question to write the reply (no account details are sent).
  • Vercel hosts the app; Neon hosts the database; OpenStreetMap provides map data; Google Fonts provides fonts.

Your choices

  • Delete any chat from the history list.
  • Delete all your data at any time from My account – this removes chats, checks, reminders and your account.
  • Guests are identified only by a random cookie; clearing cookies disconnects that history.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact form.
