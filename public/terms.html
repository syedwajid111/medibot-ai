Not medical advice

MediBot provides general health information for education only. It is not a doctor, does not diagnose, and does not replace professional medical advice, examination or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

Emergencies

Do not use MediBot in an emergency. Call 1122 or 115, or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

Medicines

Medicine information is general. Follow the label and the advice of your doctor or pharmacist. The interaction checker covers only well-known combinations and is not complete.

AI-generated text

Some replies are written by an AI model (Groq) from MediBot's knowledge base. AI can make mistakes – check important information with a professional. Replies marked "General AI guidance" are not from the verified library.

Acceptable use

Don't misuse the service, attempt to break it, or submit other people's personal data. We may limit requests to keep the service available.

Project status

MediBot is a university final-year project provided "as is", without warranties.
